# Ask the Model v0.7.1

Released: 2026-10-14

**Chart presentation and chart-only response corrections**

## Summary

v0.7.1 is a compatible patch following acceptance testing of v0.7.0. It fixes two presentation defects affecting the already-qualified GISTEMP v1 and Energy Institute TES v2 chart routes. It does not add a chart source or broaden chart qualification.

## Fixes

- Give transcript-embedded chart widgets enough natural width for the qualified Energy Institute footer to render, instead of showing the renderer fallback “Chart needs more space” when the complete admitted series is available.
- Make chart-only GISTEMP and Energy Institute responses concise and deterministic. The transcript no longer substitutes a short list or pseudo-table of values from incomplete retrieval excerpts, nor claims that the chart cannot be rendered or that additional years are unavailable based only on those excerpts.
- Direct users to the native Chart/Data view for the admitted values and source provenance.
- Preserve grounded model responses for prompts explicitly asking for interpretation, year-specific values or a table.

## Verification

- Maintainer-confirmed acceptance on elementary OS 8 for both Energy Institute and GISTEMP chart rendering and chart-only transcript responses.
- All 13 workflows passed on the exact PR #469 head before merge.
- The Flatpak/Meson suite passed 108/108 tests, including the registered `chart-intent` regression and GTK chart-view checks.

## Scope and qualification boundaries

- Application charting remains limited to the explicitly qualified GISTEMP v1 and Energy Institute TES v2 profiles from pinned EWD snapshots.
- The Energy Institute chart reflects the admitted processed EWD snapshot; this release does not claim independent reproduction of the upstream raw-to-processed pipeline.
- BNR C01 remains native/test-only without an application chart route. M4 reduction remains dormant.
- Arbitrary sources, model-authored numeric chart data, general multi-series/derived charts and generalized missing/event/discontinuity semantics are not introduced. Broader CHART-01 qualification remains open in issue #343.
- Optimizations remain session-only and default OFF.
